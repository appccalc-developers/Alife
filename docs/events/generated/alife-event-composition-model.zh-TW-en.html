  2. 強制政策與禁用規則Mandatory policy and prohibitions
  3. 仍合法的人工選擇／明確關閉Still-valid human selections and explicit closes

required · recommended · selected · inactive · exceptionRequested · exceptionApproved

活動事實變更時

  1. 重新執行政策和模組判斷，但不立即修改現有方案。
  2. 產生差異：新增／移除模組、角色、權限、任務、審批和資料要求。
  3. 顯示影響和阻塞，例如「新增兒童後需要兒童安全負責人及兩名合資格助教」。
  4. 由有權限的人確認；不能自動授予角色、指定審批人或發布。
  5. 保存新版本和原版本，確保歷史決定可追溯。
  6. 若模組已有資料、付款、提交或審批，移除要求轉為阻塞式退休流程，不能靜默刪除。

When event facts change

  1. Re-evaluate policy and modules without immediately mutating the current plan.
  2. Produce a diff of modules, roles, access, tasks, approvals and data requirements added or removed.
  3. Explain impact and blockers—for example, “adding children requires a safeguarding lead and two eligible assistants”.
  4. An authorised person confirms the change; the system cannot grant roles, appoint approvers or publish automatically.
  5. Retain both old and new versions so historical decisions remain traceable.
  6. If a module already owns data, payments, submissions or approvals, removal becomes a blocking retirement workflow and never a silent delete.
